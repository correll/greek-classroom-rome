# Places for illustrations

An index of the empty space in the printed book, with a suggestion for
what could fill each one. It was measured on the build of October 2026
(student edition 387 pages, teacher's edition 536). If the text changes, run
the measurement again (see the end of this file).

**How to read the table.** *Page* is the printed page number in the student
edition. *Space* is the empty height at the foot of the page, in inches,
first in the student edition and then at the same point in the teacher's
edition. The text block is 5.3 in wide, so a picture can always be the full
width. A picture fits **both** editions if it is no taller than the smaller
of the two figures, less about 0.3 in for spacing. Where the teacher's
figure is small, the picture can be printed in the student edition only.

**Why chapter ends are safe.** Every lesson starts on a new page. A picture
placed in the space at the end of a lesson cannot move anything in any other
lesson. The spaces in the middle of a lesson (listed separately below) are
different: a picture there pushes everything after it down the page.

**Style.** One hand throughout works best. A suggestion is pen-and-ink line
drawing with a single wash in the book's terracotta or ink blue, in the
spirit of a teacher's sketch on a wax tablet. Avoid imitating any particular
illustrator's style. Where a real object is named below (a coin, a papyrus,
a fresco), it is in the public domain and can be drawn from a museum
photograph.

page. A picture placed there moves everything after it in the lesson, so
the space may close up or move once something is inserted. Each would suit
a teaching diagram rather than a scene.

## Re-measuring

The figures come from rendering each page of `build/greek-classroom.pdf` and
`build/greek-classroom-teacher.pdf` and finding the last row of ink above
the footer. The text block runs from 0.95 in to 9.0 in from the top of a
10 in page. A space of 1.8 in or more was counted.
