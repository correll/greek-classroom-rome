# Attic and Koine Face to Face

::: {.story time="5 min"}
Felix, who has spent twenty-two lessons preferring real speech to formal grammar, is finally vindicated: the Greek of the New Testament is closer to the Greek of sailors than to the Greek of Plato, and he can hear it.

**Grammar for this lesson.** Systematic comparison: vocabulary, the dual and optative receding, **ἵνα** clauses replacing the infinitive, simplified sentence structure, the dative under pressure.
