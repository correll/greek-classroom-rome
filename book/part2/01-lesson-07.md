# The Article's Many Disguises

::: {.story time="5 min"}
Julia catches Marcus assigning the wrong article to a noun he has known for a fortnight. Marcus defends himself on the grounds that the article is decorative. Theodoros gives him a sentence in which the article is the only thing distinguishing two readings.

> **To be written.** The scene above is the plan; the prose is not yet
> drafted. See the course notes in `README.md` for the drafting order.
:::

::: {.newgreek time="10 min"}
**Grammar for this lesson.** The full article: **ὁ ἡ τό** in all three genders, singular and plural, nominative and accusative; the article as a signpost; basic accent rules for nouns.

> **To be written.** Present one main grammatical idea with a paradigm
> table, two or three worked examples, and a **From Latin** box wherever
> the Latin parallel either helps or misleads.
:::

::: vocab
**τὰ γράμματα** letters, writing · **ὁ χρόνος** time · **ἡ ἡμέρα** day · **τὸ ἔργον** work, deed · **πολλοί** many · **μόνος** only, alone

> **To be checked.** Accentuation, vowel length, and the article for every
> noun must be verified against a lexicon before this lesson is taught.
:::
