::: {.voice time="10 min"}
**Text for this lesson.** An Aesopic fable, first in an adapted version using only known forms, then the authentic opening of the same fable for comparison. The pupils see exactly what was taken out and why.

> **To be selected and verified.** The passage must be quoted exactly from
> a public-domain edition and cited. Vocabulary beyond the course list must
> be glossed in full. Do not present a composed sentence as a quotation.
:::

::: {.question time="5 min"}
You read an adapted fable and then the real one. What was lost in the adaptation? Was the adaptation dishonest, or necessary, or both?
:::
