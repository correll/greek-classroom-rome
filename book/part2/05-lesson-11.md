::: {.voice time="10 min"}
**Text for this lesson.** A short passage in which the subject changes without ever being named, carried entirely by verb endings — ordinary in Greek, impossible in English.

> **To be selected and verified.** The passage must be quoted exactly from

> be glossed in full. Do not present a composed sentence as a quotation.

::: {.question time="5 min"}
