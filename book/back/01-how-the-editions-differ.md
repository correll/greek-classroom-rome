# A Note on the Two Editions {.unnumbered}

Two PDFs are built from this source.

**The student edition** contains every lesson, every exercise, and every
text, and no answers at all. It also contains no cast list, no character
biographies, and no lesson-by-lesson summary of what happens — because the
characters are meant to be discovered through the lessons, not looked up in
advance.

**The teacher's edition** adds three things: each exercise is followed
immediately by its answer key, with notes on the mistakes students usually
make and why those mistakes are reasonable; a chapter of character notes
saying what each pupil is for and which lesson reveals what; and the full
thirty-lesson scope and sequence, including the story of each lesson.

Both are generated from the same source files by the same command, with one
flag changed. They cannot drift apart, and an exercise cannot be revised in
one edition without being revised in the other.

If you are holding the student edition and want the answers, the file is
`greek-classroom-teacher.pdf` in the same place you found this one — though
if you are a student, reading the character notes first will cost you
something the book was trying to give you.
