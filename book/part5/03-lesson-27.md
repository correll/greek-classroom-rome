# The Translator's Dilemma

::: {.story time="5 min"}
Two translations of the same sentence compete. Both are defensible on the grammar. Only one is right, and the reason has nothing to do with endings.

> **To be written.** The scene above is the plan; the prose is not yet
> drafted. See the course notes in `README.md` for the drafting order.
:::

::: {.newgreek time="10 min"}
**Grammar for this lesson.** Ambiguity, idiom, and semantic range; why a grammatically possible translation can still be wrong; what a translator is actually choosing between.

> **To be written.** Present one main grammatical idea with a paradigm
> table, two or three worked examples, and a **From Latin** box wherever
> the Latin parallel either helps or misleads.
