# The Grammar of Questions

::: {.story time="5 min"}
The pupils write questions for Theodoros, who has agreed to answer any question that is correctly formed in Greek. The class discovers that a badly formed question is its own punishment.

> **To be written.** The scene above is the plan; the prose is not yet
> drafted. See the course notes in `README.md` for the drafting order.
:::

::: {.newgreek time="10 min"}
**Grammar for this lesson.** Interrogative pronouns and adverbs (**τίς**, **τί**, **ποῦ**, **πῶς**, **διὰ τί**); the accent that distinguishes interrogative from indefinite; direct and indirect questions.

> **To be written.** Present one main grammatical idea with a paradigm
> table, two or three worked examples, and a **From Latin** box wherever
> the Latin parallel either helps or misleads.
:::

::: vocab
**τίς, τί** who? what? · **ποῦ** where? · **πῶς** how? · **διὰ τί** why? · **πότε** when? · **ἀποκρίνομαι** I answer

> **To be checked.** Accentuation, vowel length, and the article for every
> noun must be verified against a lexicon before this lesson is taught.
:::
