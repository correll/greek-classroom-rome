# The Trial of a Translation

::: {.story time="5 min"}
Each pupil defends a translation before the class. Marcus's is elegant and wrong; Julia's is correct and delivered so quietly that nobody hears it. Theodoros instructs the judges to distinguish evidence from eloquence, which turns out to be the hardest instruction of the term.
