# Odysseus and the Problem of Cleverness

::: {.story time="5 min"}
An episode from the *Odyssey* in which Odysseus lies successfully. Theodosius is troubled by this; Quintus defends it; Sabina points out that the poem seems to admire him for it. Theodoros refuses to settle the question.
