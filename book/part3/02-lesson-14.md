# The Aorist Arrives

::: {.story time="5 min"}
Marcus declares that one past tense ought to be enough for anyone and offers to prove it. Theodoros gives him two sentences that differ only in aspect and asks him to translate both into Latin without losing anything. He cannot.

> **To be written.** The scene above is the plan; the prose is not yet

:::

::: {.newgreek time="10 min"}
**Grammar for this lesson.** Aorist indicative active, first (sigmatic) and second; aspect versus tense; why Greek has more than one past.

> **To be written.** Present one main grammatical idea with a paradigm

**Text for this lesson.** A pair of authentic sentences, one imperfect and one aorist, describing the same kind of action — the clearest demonstration in the language that the distinction is real.

> **To be selected and verified.** The passage must be quoted exactly from

::: {.question time="5 min"}
