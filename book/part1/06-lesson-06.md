::: {.newgreek time="10 min"}
**Grammar for this lesson.** Consolidation: article + noun + verb; negation with **οὐ**; reading a whole sentence before translating any of it.

> **To be written.** Present one main grammatical idea with a paradigm
> table, two or three worked examples, and a **From Latin** box wherever
> the Latin parallel either helps or misleads.
:::

::: vocab
Review; no new vocabulary. A cumulative list of all words from Lessons 1-5 is drilled.

> **To be checked.** Accentuation, vowel length, and the article for every
> noun must be verified against a lexicon before this lesson is taught.
:::

::: {.voice time="10 min"}
**Text for this lesson.** A genuine short sentence from an Attic author, selected so that every form in it has been taught. The pupils discover they can read Greek without knowing every word in Greek.

> **To be selected and verified.** The passage must be quoted exactly from
> a public-domain edition and cited. Vocabulary beyond the course list must
> be glossed in full. Do not present a composed sentence as a quotation.
