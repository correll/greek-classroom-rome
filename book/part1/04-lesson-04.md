# Who Did What?

::: {.story time="5 min"}
Sabina stages a courtroom. Two pupils are accused of the same act and the class must determine, from the Greek alone, which of them did it. Sabina keeps rearranging the words to see whether the verdict changes. It does not, which annoys her and delights Theodoros.

::: {.voice time="10 min"}
**Text for this lesson.** A short Attic sentence with the object placed first for emphasis, glossed word by word. Candidate: a one-line maxim from Menander's *Monostichoi*, the standard schoolroom text of this period.

> **To be selected and verified.** The passage must be quoted exactly from
> a public-domain edition and cited. Vocabulary beyond the course list must
> be glossed in full. Do not present a composed sentence as a quotation.

::: {.question time="5 min"}
If moving the words does not change who is guilty, what *does* the order change? When Sabina puts the accused first, what is she doing to her audience?
