# Adjectives

Adjectives agree with their noun in gender, number, and case. Most belong to the first and second declensions; a substantial group belongs to the third.

## Planned subsections

1. Adjectives of the first and second declension: **ἀγαθός, ἀγαθή, ἀγαθόν**
2. Two-termination adjectives: **ἄδικος, ἄδικον**, chiefly compounds
3. Contract adjectives: **χρυσοῦς, χρυσῆ, χρυσοῦν**
4. Third-declension adjectives: **εὐδαίμων**, **ἀληθής**, **πᾶς**, **μέγας**, **πολύς**
5. Attributive and predicate position (see §3.4)
6. The adjective used as a noun: **οἱ ἀγαθοί**, **τὸ καλόν**
7. Comparison: **-τερος, -τατος** and **-ίων, -ιστος**
8. Irregular comparison: **ἀγαθός**, **κακός**, **μέγας**, **πολύς**, **μικρός**
9. The genitive of comparison and the construction with **ἤ**
