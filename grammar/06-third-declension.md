# The Third Declension

Nouns whose stem ends in a consonant or in **ι/υ**. The largest and least regular declension, and the one that makes or breaks a second-year reader.

## Planned subsections

1. The stem and how to find it: the genitive singular, not the nominative
2. Endings common to all consonant stems
3. Labial, velar, and dental stems; the dative plural in **-σι** and what it does to the stem (§1.6)
4. Liquid and nasal stems: **ὁ ῥήτωρ**, **ὁ δαίμων**, **ὁ ἀγών**
5. Neuter stems in **-ματ-**: **τὸ ὄνομα**, **τὸ σῶμα**, **τὸ πρᾶγμα**
6. Sigma stems: **τὸ γένος**, **ἡ τριήρης**, and the contractions they produce
7. Stems in **ι** and **υ**: **ἡ πόλις**, **ὁ πῆχυς**
8. Irregular high-frequency nouns: **ὁ ἀνήρ**, **ὁ πατήρ**, **ἡ μήτηρ**, **ἡ γυνή**, **ἡ ναῦς**, **ὁ παῖς**, **ὁ Ζεύς**
9. The dual, in all three declensions
