2. Ordinals; the alphabetic numeral system and the three obsolete letters it preserves (§1.1)
3. Adverbs formed from adjectives: **-ως** from the genitive plural
4. Adverbs of place, time, and manner; correlative series (**ποῦ / ποι / πόθεν**)
5. The negatives **οὐ** and **μή**: which goes where, and what **οὐ μή** does
6. Connective particles: **δέ**, **γάρ**, **οὖν**, **ἀλλά**, **καί**, **τε**
7. **μέν … δέ** and the architecture of a Greek sentence
8. Modal and emphatic particles: **ἄν**, **γε**, **δή**, **που**, **τοι**, **μέντοι**
