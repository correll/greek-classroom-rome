# The Verb: Tense Formation

How each tense stem is built from the verb's principal parts, and what happens when a stem meets a suffix.

## Planned subsections

1. The present and imperfect: the present stem, thematic vowel, and primary and secondary endings
2. Contract verbs in **-άω**, **-έω**, **-όω**, and the contraction rules
3. The future: **-σ-** added to the stem, and the consonant changes it causes (§1.6)
4. Liquid and nasal stems: the future in **-έω** with contraction; **μένω → μενῶ**, **ἀγγέλλω → ἀγγελῶ**
5. The first (sigmatic) aorist and the second (strong) aorist; why some verbs have one and some the other
6. The perfect and pluperfect; reduplication; the first and second perfect
7. The passive: aorist passive in **-θη-**, future passive
8. The **-μι** verbs: **δίδωμι**, **τίθημι**, **ἵστημι**, **δείκνυμι**
9. Irregular principal parts worth memorising at once: **εἰμί**, **ἔρχομαι**, **φέρω**, **λέγω**, **ὁράω**, **οἶδα**
