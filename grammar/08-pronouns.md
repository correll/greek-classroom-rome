# Pronouns

Greek pronouns are mostly irregular, mostly frequent, and mostly worth memorising outright.

## Planned subsections

1. Personal pronouns: **ἐγώ**, **σύ**, and the third person supplied by **αὐτός**
2. The emphatic and unemphatic forms, and when the subject pronoun is used at all
3. **αὐτός** in its three distinct uses: *self*, *same*, and *him/her/it*
4. Demonstratives: **οὗτος**, **ὅδε**, **ἐκεῖνος**, and how they differ
5. The relative pronoun **ὅς, ἥ, ὅ**; attraction of the relative
6. Interrogative **τίς, τί** and indefinite **τις, τι**: the accent is the whole difference (§2.6)
7. Reflexive pronouns: **ἐμαυτοῦ**, **σεαυτοῦ**, **ἑαυτοῦ**
8. Reciprocal **ἀλλήλων**; possessive adjectives; **ὅστις** and the indefinite relative
