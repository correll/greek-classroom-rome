The masculine and neuter follow the second declension; the feminine follows
the first declension in **-η**. After **ε**, **ι**, or **ρ** the feminine
has **-ᾱ**: **δικαίᾱ**, **μικρᾱ́**.

## Verb: λύω *I loose, release*

Present, imperfect, and aorist indicative active — the three tenses taught
in Volume I.

| | **Present** | **Imperfect** | **Aorist** |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 sg. | **λύω** | **ἔλυον** | **ἔλυσα** |
| 2 sg. | **λύεις** | **ἔλυες** | **ἔλυσας** |
| 3 sg. | **λύει** | **ἔλυε(ν)** | **ἔλυσε(ν)** |
| 1 pl. | **λύομεν** | **ἐλύομεν** | **ἐλύσαμεν** |
| 2 pl. | **λύετε** | **ἐλύετε** | **ἐλύσατε** |
| 3 pl. | **λύουσι(ν)** | **ἔλυον** | **ἔλυσαν** |

Note that the first singular and third plural of the imperfect are
identical (**ἔλυον**). Only the context distinguishes them. This is true of
every thematic verb.

Infinitives: present **λύειν** · aorist **λῦσαι**.

## Verb: εἰμί *I am*

| | **Present** | **Imperfect** |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 sg. | **εἰμί** | **ἦν** (or **ἦ**) |
| 2 sg. | **εἶ** | **ἦσθα** |
| 3 sg. | **ἐστί(ν)** | **ἦν** |
| 1 pl. | **ἐσμέν** | **ἦμεν** |
| 2 pl. | **ἐστέ** | **ἦτε** |
| 3 pl. | **εἰσί(ν)** | **ἦσαν** |

Infinitive **εἶναι**. Participle **ὤν, οὖσα, ὄν**.

All present-indicative forms except **εἶ** are enclitic (§2.8). **ἐστί**
takes an accent on its first syllable (**ἔστι**) when it means *exists* or
stands at the beginning of a sentence.
